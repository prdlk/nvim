# Identify job stories

Generate job stories for the scenario, project, or system described below.

## Steps

1. If the scenario is an existing product or codebase, read its docs and relevant code to find the real workflows and constraints.
2. Map the stakeholders and how they relate, the touchpoints between them and the system, constraints, and success criteria. Look beyond the obvious users to people who maintain, operate, audit, or are affected by the system.
3. Look for hidden needs: motivations stakeholders would not state directly, and how context (time pressure, uncertainty, expertise) changes what they need.
4. Write job stories across: first use, routine operation, exceptions and edge cases, maintenance and change over time, data and information flow, integrations with other systems, and learning or recovery. Vary the time horizon, complexity, emotional state and expertise level.
5. Chain related stories where one is a prerequisite or follow-up of another.

## Output

- Job stories in the form: When <situation>, I want to <motivation>, so I can <outcome>. Group them by theme and give each a verb label (for example DISCOVER, VALIDATE) when it helps. Write as many as the scenario supports, usually 15 to 30; skip filler.
- Task chains: any sequences of stories that form a workflow.
- Hidden needs: a short list of non-obvious needs the stories uncovered.
